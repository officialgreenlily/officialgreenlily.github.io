About

Reagan Land, piano teacher

I’ve played the piano for 16 years, and I know how easily lessons can take the fun out of learning. Too often it becomes about drills, grades and pieces you never chose.

My lessons are different. We start with the music you actually want to play and build the skills along the way, at a pace that suits you. If you’d like to write your own songs, we can work on that too: chords, melody and putting your ideas together.
